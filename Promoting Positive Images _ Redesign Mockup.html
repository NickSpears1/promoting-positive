Who we are

Treating every person with dignity, respect, and compassion.

Through counseling, mentorship, and community engagement, PPI helps individuals and families recognize their strengths, build confidence, and develop the skills to navigate life’s challenges.

Our Mission

To strengthen individuals, families, and communities through accessible mental health counseling, mentorship, and supportive programs that promote emotional wellness, resilience, and personal growth.

Our Vision

Healthier communities where people feel empowered, supported, and confident in their ability to thrive, with less stigma around mental health.

Meet Jerome Roberson

About the Founder

Jerome is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker, educator, and community advocate, and director of PPI. He is an adjunct instructor at Eastern Michigan University and has spent 23 years working within the Belleville-area school district supporting youth, families, and community members.

His belief: meaningful change begins with recognizing individual strengths and creating places where people feel supported, respected, and empowered.

Why PPI?

Support that’s connected to your community.

Mental health support works best when it’s rooted in trust and in the community people live in. Through counseling, mentorship, education, and partnerships, you never have to face life’s challenges alone.

Common questions

What happens in a first session?

Your clinician will get to know you, talk through what brought you in, and work with you to set goals. Nothing is rushed.

Do you work with teens and children?

Yes. Youth and adolescents are a core focus, along with mentorship and school partnerships.

Is telehealth available?

Sessions can be requested in person or by telehealth in the scheduler above.

Can my school or organization partner with PPI?

Absolutely. Reach out through the contact form to discuss workshops, mentorship, and wellness programming.
